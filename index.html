【從閱讀理解邁向條理分明的段落寫作】 [Leading Students from Reading Comprehension to Well-Organized Paragraph Writing]

當學生的美語學習不再只是日常簡單對話，而是需要將想法組織成清晰、有說服力的文字時，別擔心！這個年紀的孩子超有想法，只是需要一點「酷方法」把腦中的火花變成條理分明的文章。在 Advance B1 班的 38 堂課中，老師們將以循序漸進且極具耐心的方式，引導學生告別中式英文、瓦解寫作焦慮，一步步建立完整的英文表達結構！我們將透過 Oxford Discover 3 延伸跨學科的主題閱讀，累積豐富的單字與文章理解力，並全面搭配 Azar Basic Grammar 系統化基礎文法。學生不會死背語法公式，而是直接將文法融入句型訓練與文章寫作實作中。透過「文法核心 ➔ 句型造句 ➔ 段落實作」的漸進式練習，學生將解鎖核心時態、詞性與名詞變化，讓句型結構不再出錯；同時熟練運用連接詞與複合句，並發揮初階的寫作技巧，親手寫出主題明確、語意正確的小短文。本學期預期讓學生穩健達到 CEFR B1 基礎獨立動筆（Threshold） 的初階寫作等級，能針對熟悉的主題或生活經驗，寫出結構連貫、語意清楚的簡短文章，為未來的英文大作打下超強的成就感與自信心！

【Think Deeply, Write Confidently】 When learning English is no longer just about daily chit-chat, but about organizing thoughts into clear, persuasive writing, don't worry! Students at this age have amazing ideas; they just need a cool way to turn those mental sparks into well-organized articles. In the 38 lessons of our Advance B1 class, teachers will guide students patiently and step by step. We will help them wave goodbye to "Chinglish" and writing anxiety, building a complete and solid structure for English expression! Through Oxford Discover 3, we will explore cross-curricular thematic reading to build a rich vocabulary and strong article comprehension, fully integrating Azar Basic Grammar. Instead of mindlessly memorizing grammar rules, students will apply grammar directly to sentence patterns and practical paragraph writing. Through the progressive practice of "Core Grammar ➔ Sentence Formulation ➔ Paragraph Implementation," students will master core tenses, parts of speech, and noun inflections, eliminating structural errors. They will also master the use of conjunctions and compound sentences, applying basic writing techniques to compose clear and grammatically correct short essays. This semester, students are expected to steadily reach the introductory writing proficiency of CEFR B1 (Threshold). They will be able to write connected, clear text on familiar topics or personal experiences, building ultimate confidence and a sense of achievement for their future essays!

Part 1: Interdisciplinary Topics & Reading Comprehension

  • Units 9-10: Green Energy & Energy Saving ⚡ —— 探究綠色能源與省能小尖兵故事，建立環保科學詞彙。
    Exploring green energy and conservation through engaging stories (Units 9-10).
  • Units 11-12: Mysteries & World of Instruments 🎷 —— 閱讀體育館謎案與音樂樂器世界，提升邏輯推理與文化視界。
    Solving mysteries and exploring global instruments to enhance text comprehension (Units 11-12).
  • Units 13-14: Simple Machines & Inventions 💡 —— 了解生活中的簡單機械與發明家故事，學習步驟敘述與點子表達。

  • Units 15-16: Life Science & Gardens of Happiness 🌻 —— 觀察向日葵的生命週期與幸福花園，培養自然寫作與閱讀耐力。
    Observing plant life cycles and expressive natural themes (Units 15-16).
  • Units 17-18: Magellan's Voyage & Undersea Exploration 🌊 —— 跟隨麥哲倫船長與海洋探險家登艦，拓展大航海歷史與海洋科學視野。
    Journeying with Magellan and exploring Cousteau's undersea world (Units 17-18).
